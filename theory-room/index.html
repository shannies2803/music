Questions

Is this from ABRSM or Trinity?

No. The Theory Room is independent and isn't connected to either board. It prepares students for their exams. Always check the board's own syllabus before entering for an exam.

What do we need?

A phone, tablet or computer with a web browser, and headphones or speakers. A microphone is only needed to sing answers in the aural room. Singing is marked inside your browser and never uploaded.

How many children can use one plan?

Up to three learners per family, each with their own progress. Sign in with one email and switch learners on the dashboard.

Is there a live teacher?

No. Everything is self-paced. The guided courses include a page for the grown-up with what to do together each day, and answers and marking keys.

Does it cover Trinity?

Yes for aural and repertoire: every Trinity aural test from Initial to Grade 8, and every ABRSM and Trinity exam list. The theory courses follow ABRSM; Trinity theory is planned.

Can we cancel?

Yes, any time, from the link in your receipt email. The Family plan stays open until the end of the month or year you paid for. Grade packs are a one-off payment and last 12 months.

The Theory Room is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to ABRSM or Trinity College London. Exam names and syllabuses belong to the boards.
