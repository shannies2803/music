  • ABRSM runs Grades 1–5 on demand.
  • Your child is filmed for the whole exam and can't use notes. Read ABRSM's candidate instructions for the ID and room checks before booking.
  • There is no need to sit Grades 1–4 first.
  • If their practical exams are with ABRSM, your child needs Grade 5 theory (or an accepted alternative) before your child can take it.

Printables: everything you need

  • Lesson packs: every day in the plan has a printable pack (button on the day's card) with what to say, a worked example, picture cards, questions to do together with answers, and their worksheet with an answer key.
  • Our practice papers, with a marking key and a place to record their marks:
  • Optional extras: ABRSM's own practice papers and the Discovering Music Theory workbooks are good if you want even more practice, but you don't need them.
  • Wide-stave manuscript paper. Your child should write by hand every session, even though the exam is on screen.
  • Free lessons on every ABRSM topic at My Music Theory.
  • Their own pieces for the "find it in real music" moments.

Keeping a 7-year-old going

  • Stop while your child is still enjoying it. Short and regular beats long and tired.
  • Praise explanations more than speed ("you knew why").
  • When your child guesses, ask "what rule would help us decide?" before giving the answer.
  • Fast-track: three quick correct answers on three different days makes a topic green. Stop drilling it after that.
  • A mistake leaves their list after your child gets it right twice, so the list stays short.
