Terms

The short version

  • Independent. The Theory Room is not connected to ABRSM or Trinity College London. We prepare students for their exams; we can't promise a result. Check the board's own syllabus before entering.
  • One family. A Family plan is for one household, with up to three learners. A teacher licence covers the number of students it names.
  • Family plan. Billed monthly or yearly until you cancel. Cancel any time from your receipt email or the "Change or cancel" link on your dashboard; your plan stays open to the end of the period you paid for.
  • Grade packs. One payment for 12 months of one grade's theory and aural.
  • Refunds. If something isn't working for you in the first 14 days, email us and we'll refund you.
  • Our content. The lessons, questions, music and guides are ours or used with permission. Please don't copy them to share or sell.
  • Changes. If prices change, the new price applies from your next renewal and we'll email you first.
